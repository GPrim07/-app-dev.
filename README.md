# My Favorite Series: *Jujutsu Kaisen*

**Why I love it:**  
- Intense action scenes with stunning animation.  
- Deep character development and emotional storytelling.  
- The balance of dark themes and humor makes it unique.

## Favorite Season
Season 1 — the perfect introduction to the world of curses, sorcerers, and powerful battles.

### Episode Highlights
1. Episode 12: "To You, Someday" — Yuji and Nobara’s teamwork is amazing.  
2. Episode 19: "Black Flash" — the fight choreography and animation are mind-blowing.  
3. Episode 24: "Accomplices" — a satisfying season finale showing the growth of the main trio.
